# Artwork and attribution

The launcher and optional DMG use original geometric SVG artwork with rendered native/raster assets, dedicated by yeojuny under [CC0-1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). No other distributor's installer design or image pixels were reused.

The 53 active replacements cover app/wordmarks, abstract backgrounds, mode/terrain/direction icons, pointer/resize indicators, a generic minimap and Armada/Cortex/unknown badges. Filenames, dimensions, alpha and functional meaning are preserved. The generic unknown badge is not relabeled as a faction. Editable masters and per-asset notices are in the matching source download; three dormant UNKNOWN pixels and unused sidecars are excluded.

The installer background has standard/Retina PNGs from the original SVG. Its layout has one BAR.app at 176/205 and an Applications link at 504/205 with an English drag instruction. Both DMG and ZIP were opened independently; all 1,171 app file/link entries, bytes, modes and strict ad-hoc signatures matched inventory `6f8908ed…`. The actual Finder DMG layout showed the original artwork, one BAR.app and Applications shortcut at the configured positions.

Upstream code retains BAR Lobby Authors attribution. Game-owned art/content, engine resources, audio, fonts, maps, trademarks and dependencies retain their respective licenses. Original launcher artwork does not relicense them or change gameplay. App-local third-party notices preserve Electron/Chromium, npm, font, engine/AI/downloader and native runtime terms; corresponding-source downloads remain separate.
